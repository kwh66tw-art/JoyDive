# Privacy Policy — JD2 Dive Logbook

**Last Updated**: June 3, 2026

---

## Overview

JD2 Dive Logbook ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

---

## Data We Collect

### Data You Provide (Stored Locally Only)
- Dive log entries (depth, duration, location, notes, gas mix, etc.)
- GPS coordinates you choose to record for dive sites

**All dive log data is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not transmit your dive data to any server.**

### Data Collected Automatically

#### Advertising (Non-Premium Users)
The App uses **Google AdMob** to display banner advertisements for non-premium users. AdMob may collect:
- Device advertising identifier (IDFA) — only with your permission
- IP address
- Device type and OS version
- Ad interaction data

For details, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

#### Location
The App requests location permission only when you explicitly choose to record GPS coordinates for a dive. Location data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

---

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
- We do not store your dive data on our servers
- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not track your usage across other apps or websites

---

## In-App Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase ($1.99) is processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

---

## Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## Data Retention

All dive log data is stored on your device. You can delete all data at any time via **Settings → Developer Tools → Clear All Dives**, or by deleting the App.

---

## Your Rights

You may:
- Delete all your data by removing the App
- Opt out of personalized ads by disabling tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking
- Request App Tracking Transparency permission reset via iOS Settings

We will update this policy as needed and reflect the change date above.
